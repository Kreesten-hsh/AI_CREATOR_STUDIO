# AI CREATOR STUDIO : Design Direction & Visual Identity

## Identity & Personality
- **Product**: AI CREATOR STUDIO (Interactive 25-Day Claude AI Challenge & Course Engine)
- **Aesthetic Direction**: Neo-Brutalist Editorial Print & Digital Starter Kit
- **Tone**: Bold, tactile, structured, editorial, practical, zero corporate fluff

## Color System
- **Paper Cream (`#FAF6F0`)**: Primary canvas and inactive step surface
- **Vivid Orange (`#FF4D00`)**: Primary brand accent, active/completed states, and editorial hero banners
- **Deep Black (`#111111`)**: All structural borders (`2px solid`), hard offset shadows (`4px 4px 0px #111111`), and primary ink
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Elevated interactive cards, form inputs, and floating day badges

## Typography
- **Display Headings**: `Archivo Black` / `Syne` (Bold uppercase structural headlines) paired with `Instrument Serif` italic for editorial contrast (e.g. `NICHE THE eff DOWN` / `CONCEPT breakdown`)
- **Body Copy**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (High-legibility editorial grotesque)
- **Technical / Prompts / Counters**: `JetBrains Mono` (Day counters, step indices, prompt variable tokens `{{business}}`)

## Layout & Liveliness Dials
- **ENERGY**: 3 (High tactile contrast: checkerboard panels, rotated day stamp card, crisp 2px borders, hard offset shadows with active press states)
- **RHYTHM**: 3 (Varied composition between split DayBanner, 4-step horizontal tabs, interactive widgets, and callout boxes)
- **MOTION**: 2 (Purposeful spring transitions on step switches, progress bar fills, and 25-day grid square completion)
